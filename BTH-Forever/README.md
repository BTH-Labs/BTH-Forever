# BTH Forever

### A vanilla-plus adventure built to play through with friends

</div>

---

## ✨ What is BTH Forever?

**BTH Forever** keeps the heart of vanilla Minecraft intact, then quietly expands it in every direction. A little more to build, a little more to automate, and a *lot* more to fight. 🗡️

It is tuned for **multiplayer**: light enough to run on a small server with friends, packed with quality-of-life so nobody is fumbling with recipes, and deep enough that everyone can carve out their own role. One of you runs the farm, one builds the factory, one goes boss hunting. 🤝

The world still looks and plays like the Minecraft you know. It just has a lot more worth doing.

---

## 🌍 Vanilla, but richer

The overworld is reshaped into something worth exploring without ever losing its familiar feel.

- 🏔️ **Terralith** and **Geophilic** turn worldgen into sweeping, varied landscapes
- 🏚️ **YUNG's** overhauled dungeons, mineshafts, and strongholds make the places you already know feel brand new
- 🧭 **Nature's Compass** and **Explorer's Compass** help you and your friends actually find that biome or structure
- 🐾 **Naturalist** and **Friends & Foes** fill the world with more wildlife and mobs

## 🍳 Cozy

Settle in. There is always something to grow, cook, or decorate.

- 🥘 **Farmer's Delight** and the full **Let's Do** collection (Vinery, Meadow, Bakery, Hearth & Timber and more)
- 🪑 **Supplementaries** and the complete **Macaw's** suite (furniture, roofs, bridges, doors, windows...)
- 🎒 **Sophisticated Storage & Backpacks** so your base stays tidy
- 🍎 **Spice of Life**, **Eating Animation**, and **AppleSkin** for a richer food loop

## ⚙️ Tech & automation

When you are ready to go bigger, the gears are waiting. 🔧

- 🏗️ **Create** and its many addons (Aeronautics, Diesel Generators, Dragons+, Enchantment Industry and more) for sprawling contraptions
- 🔩 **Oritech** and **PneumaticCraft** for deeper machine progression
- 📦 **Refined Storage** for large-scale digital storage and crafting
- 🌱 **Mystical Agriculture** to farm your way to any resource

## 💀 Bosses & combat

For the nights you would rather swing a sword than turn a crank.

- 🐉 **Bosses Rise** adds powerful new boss encounters
- 🔮 **Forbidden Arcanus** brings dark magic, rituals, and challenge
- 🏺 **Relics** rewards exploration with powerful gear

---

## 🚀 Optimized

No setup headaches. Drop in and play. 🎮

- ⚡ **Sodium**, **Iris**, **Lithium**, and **ModernFix** for strong performance
- 🔭 **Distant Horizons** for gorgeous, far-reaching view distances
- 📖 **EMI** recipe and crafting helper
- 🔍 **Jade** for at-a-glance block and entity info
- 🗺️ **Xaero's Minimap & World Map** so nobody gets lost
- 💀 **Gravestone** so a bad fight does not cost you everything
